# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Telltale welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and
experience levels. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, give
specific technical feedback, and assume good faith while evidence is gathered.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or repeatedly disrupting technical discussion. Vehicle identifiers,
diagnostic transcripts, credentials, and private correspondence must not be
shared without permission.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close threads, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors and keep work productive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ImL1s). Do not open a public
issue that identifies the people involved or reproduces private material.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Maintainers
will consider context, severity, repeated behaviour, safety, and the needs of
affected people before deciding on a response. Retaliation against someone who
reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
